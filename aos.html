An agentic AI copilot for Chartered Accountants

AOS (Accounting Operating System) is an AI-powered operating platform for CA practices. The AI Engine is its copilot and agent-orchestration layer: it plans multi-step work, calls tools through a safety boundary, and answers with grounded, client-scoped responses.

View the code Contact

What it does

Multi-step workflows

GST reconciliation, bank classification, audit document checks and monthly closing, planned and executed as agent workflows.

Tool use with a safety boundary

Consequential actions pause for confirmation before they run. Documents are treated as data, never instructions, so prompt injection doesn't reach the tools.

Client data isolation

Strict per-client scope enforced at every layer; the engine talks to the business backend over authenticated APIs and never touches the database directly.

Voice and streaming

Voice chat with speech-to-text, and Server-Sent Events that stream thinking, tool events and tokens as work happens.

Honest status: AOS is an early-stage prototype in active development. The AI Engine repo is public on GitHub; the frontend and business backend are in private development. There are no customers, revenue, funding or partnerships yet, and nothing on this page should be read as a launched product.
